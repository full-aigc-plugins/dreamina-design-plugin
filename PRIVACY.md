# Privacy

Dreamina Design is a local Codex plugin foundation. It does not include telemetry, advertising, or a hosted data service.

Future capabilities may read user-selected project files or invoke locally installed applications and CLIs. They must keep access within the user-approved scope, must not persist credentials in this repository, and must document any external data transfer before release.

Generated previews, receipts, logs, and downloaded artifacts remain under paths selected by the user. Users should review third-party product privacy terms before enabling integrations.
